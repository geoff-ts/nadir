## Methods

- Planet positions: E. M. Standish, "Approximate Positions of the Planets",
  JPL Solar System Dynamics, Table 1.
- Moon position: a short series after Jean Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms*.

These are published methods, reimplemented in `src/c/astro.c`; no code from
them is included.
